12.7 · Gradients

Five gradients, no others.

The lamp, the dusk, the plaster, the foil and the veil — as CSS tokens in colour-variables.css and as PNG textures in 05_GRAPHIC_ASSETS/Textures. Gradients never touch type.

Forbidden. Gradients on text, chromatic light (magenta / cyan), sparkles, 3D bevels, drop shadows on type, any gradient other than these five.
12.8 · Proportions and approved combinations

70 · 25 · 5.

The hierarchy bar, the seven colour rules and the nineteen approved foreground/background pairs with their WCAG 2.2 ratios from colour-tokens.json.

70 · Gallery Black / Night
25 · Plaster / white type
5

On a dark piece: about 70 % ground, 25 % type and surfaces, 5 % gold. On a light piece the ground is Plaster and the gold shrinks further — a point, an eyebrow, one lit word. If gold covers more than a line, there is too much of it.

  1. One light source. One gold accent per composition (Lamp Gold on dark, Old Gold on light). Never Lamp Gold and Ochre together.
  2. Dark is the gallery, cream is the label. Screens, social, video and covers are dark-first; documents, print and reading surfaces are Plaster / Paper.
  3. Gold is never body text on dark below 16 px, and on light only as Gold Ink.
  4. Black is Gallery Black on screen and 100 K (rich black for large solids) in print.
  5. Functional colours are for states only.
  6. No tints of gold. Opacity on Gallery White (10 %, 6 %) or Soot (10 %, 5.5 %) for hairlines.
  7. Metallic print: Old Gold as metallic spot (871 C / 872 C, verify) or foil; on screen the foil gradient is for mockups only.

12.9 · Print specifications

Ink, foil and the Pantone caveat.

From Colour_Palette.pdf and Colour_Reference.md. Gold in print is Old Gold; Lamp Gold exists only on screens.

ColourProcess CMYKNearest PantoneNote
Gallery Black0 0 0 100 · rich black 60 40 40 100 for solidsBlack 6 C (approximate)Type and small shapes in 100 K only; rich black for covers, backdrops, folders
Night13 13 0 94Black 6 C (approximate)Plates and tiles; in print prefer Gallery Black unless a visible step is wanted
Plaster0 2 6 49224 C (close)Or an uncoated natural-white stock with no ink
Soot0 8 15 90Neutral Black C (close)Warm black for text on Plaster
Old Gold0 27 68 287510 C (ΔE 5.4, close) · metallic 871 C / 872 CThe print gold: process, spot or foil. Proof on the chosen stock
Gold Ink0 30 74 467557 C (no reliable match)Gold text on light; specify as process
Lamp Gold0 24 61 97407 C (approximate)Screen only; in print it becomes Old Gold
  1. CMYK values are process conversions from sRGB and must be proofed with the printer; the kit’s print_pdf.py converts with Ghostscript.
  2. Pantone references are nearest matches by colour distance against published sRGB approximations, not a Pantone library. Verify against a physical swatch book before specifying a spot colour. ΔE and reliability are carried in colour-tokens.json.
  3. Foil. The three points of the wordmark (A, Z, D) may be hot-foiled in gold on business cards and covers; the letters stay in ink or blind-debossed, and the Z’s ring stays open to the stock.
  4. Stock. 350 gsm uncoated natural white for cards; uncoated or silk for documents; matt laminate for anything black.
  5. Never tints of gold, gradients, glow effects or gloss on black panels in print.
