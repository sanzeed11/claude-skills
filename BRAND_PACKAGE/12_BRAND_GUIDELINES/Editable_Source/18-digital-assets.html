18.2 · Open Graph and share images

One card for every page.

Share cards at 1200×630 (and 1200×1200) for every page of sanzeed.com, rendered from HTML on brand.css; generic share images in 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 in Social_Sharing.

Rule. Key content inside the centre 1200×600; the wordmark on ≥ 80 % black; title in Fraunces 300; place · idea line as the wall label. New case studies: add a line to the WORK list in build_og.py and run it.
