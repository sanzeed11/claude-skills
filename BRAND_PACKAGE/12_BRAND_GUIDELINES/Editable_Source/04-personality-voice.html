4.1 · Personality and voice

Five traits, seven principles.

Archetype: the Creator tempered by the Sage. Use this page as the pre-flight check for any piece of writing or design.

Calm.

Space, one accent, short sentences. Nothing shouts.

Precise.

Numbered steps, named deliverables, 001 counters, wall labels.

Monumental.

A large light serif, a single object on a dark field.

Warm.

Gold light, first person, “the first conversation is always free”.

Curious.

“Every environment begins as a question.”

The voice in one line

A museum’s wall label, written by the person who built the room.

Seven principles
  1. First person, singular. “I”, “me”, “my”. The brand is one person.
  2. Short sentences, long ideas. One thought per sentence. Let a full stop land.
  3. Name the deliverable. Say what the client receives, concretely.
  4. Spatial vocabulary. Rooms, routes, thresholds, galleries, light, dwell time, opening night.
  5. End on the image. Finish paragraphs on a picture, not a claim.
  6. Plain numbers. Numerals, no inflation. “90 projects, one continuous craft.”
  7. Warm, not casual. Hospitable; never slangy or jokey.
Three tests for any new copy. 1 · Could this be read aloud, calmly, in a museum lobby? 2 · Does it name a room, a route, a light or a deliverable? 3 · Would deleting the adjectives make it stronger? If yes, delete them.
