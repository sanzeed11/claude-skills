20.2 · Letterhead and envelopes

The lockup at twenty millimetres.

A4 and US Letter letterheads (SVG print masters + DOCX templates), DL and C5 envelopes; print-ready CMYK PDFs beside each. 09_PRINT_STATIONERY/Letterheads, Envelopes.

DOCX templates. Different first-page and continuation headers, footer contact lines with Page X of Y, named styles (Title = Fraunces 28, Heading 2 = Fraunces 18, Heading 3 = Inter Tight 11, Body = Inter 10/1.45), margins 20 mm. Install the fonts first.
20.3 · Documents

Invoice, quotation, proposal, statement.

Four A4 DOCX templates on the letterhead header and footer, with the confirmed promises (“I reply within two working days”) and bracketed fields. 09_PRINT_STATIONERY/Invoices, Quotations, Proposals, Official_Document.

Fields, never fake values. [GSTIN], [PAN], [Bank name], [Account no.], [IFSC], [UPI ID], [Postal address], [Client], [Fee] are visibly bracketed; Sanzeed fills them. Nothing in a template invents a price, an address or a term.
Tables. Inter Tight Medium with tabular numerals, Stone header row, hairline rows; Subtotal / GST 18 % / Total in the invoice. Currency ₹ with Indian grouping.
Export. From Word or LibreOffice to PDF; the delivered PDFs were exported headless with the fonts embedded. Office apps use the static Fraunces Light instance (no optical-size axis).
20.4 · Covers, folder, notepad, ID card, stamp, thank-you

The rest of the desk.

Each with its SVG master, trim PDF, print-ready CMYK PDF and previews; the ID card with its lanyard strip; the stamps as outlined single-colour artwork.

20.5 · Presentation template

Fourteen layouts on black.

16:9 PPTX (13.333 × 7.5 in): slide master on Gallery Black, Fraunces Light / Inter Tight SemiBold / Inter, one built slide per layout — duplicate a slide to reuse it. 09_PRINT_STATIONERY/Presentation_Template.

Install the fonts from 04_TYPOGRAPHY/Font_Files and pick Fraunces Light, Inter Tight SemiBold and Inter in the font menu. Backgrounds, veiled images and the portrait are baked assets in src/assets; the deck opens in PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides and LibreOffice (proof PDF beside it).
