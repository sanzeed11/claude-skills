21.1 · Campaign key visuals

Three lines, three ratios.

Each key visual in 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 (PNG at 2× + JPG), from HTML sources on brand.css. 10_MARKETING_COLLATERAL/Campaign_Templates, master build_campaign.py.

Composition rule, every piece. Black field (Plaster for the light banners) · one lit object or one lit room inside a rounded room plate with a hairline · headline in Fraunces 300, sentence case, ending with a full stop — the stop is the single gold point · wall-label caption · primary wordmark top-left · sanzeed.com in the footer.
21.2 · Digital ads and web banners

Seven sizes, two grounds.

The IAB set (300×250, 728×90, 160×600, 300×600, 970×250, 320×50) plus the 1080 square, and promotional web banners in dark and light.

Below 300 px wide the simplified small-size wordmark is used; below 120 px the symbol alone. The 320×50 strip carries the symbol and one line.

21.3 · Posters, flyers, brochures

Paper at every size.

Three posters at A2 and A3, the A5 flyer and DL leaflet, the A4 trifold and the 12-page company profile — bleed PDFs and print-ready CMYK PDFs beside the HTML sources.

21.4 · Events and exhibition

The room, temporarily.

Invitation (print, digital, email), backdrop, welcome board, name badges, lanyard, table tent, roll-up and booth wall. Scaled artwork carries its scale in the file name; print at 1000 % / 500 %.
