14.2 · Patterns

Seven seamless tiles.

Dot grid (two densities), beam lines, arch repeat, route, monogram repeat and S-curve, each in dark ink (Gallery White + Lamp Gold) and light ink (Soot + Old Gold). 05_GRAPHIC_ASSETS/Patterns.

Rule of use. Behind content at ≤ 12 % opacity. The tiles are drawn at full strength so the repeat is visible here; dim them in the layout. One tile per composition. Never on gold.
Files. svg/ (production tiles, 256 or 512 units), src/ (with the dashed tile guide for Illustrator / Figma), png/ (2048 px, seamless, verified), patterns.css (.pat-<name>--dark / --light as data-URI backgrounds).
Edit. Every tile is a small drawing function in build_patterns.py — change a number, re-run; or edit src/<tile>.svg inside the dashed rect.
14.3 · Textures

Grain, paper, lamp, vignette.

Procedural and deterministic (generate_textures.py). Grain belongs on dark only and is invisible on pure #000 — put it on Night, Charcoal, the dusk gradient or the lamp.

Layer order on a dark cover. Gallery Black → lamp glow (behind the one lit object) → grain, Soft Light 7 % → vignette if photography sits beneath → the object → type → the wordmark in its clear space.
14.4 · Illustrations

The monoline drawing language.

Every drawing speaks one line language: one even stroke on the drawing grid, round caps and joins, gold points sized to the stroke (r = 0.7 × stroke); labels outlined from the brand fonts. 05_GRAPHIC_ASSETS/Illustrations, master build_illustrations.py.

Seven service glyphs · 128 grid, stroke 6, point r 4.2
14.5 · Decorative elements

Dividers, badges, marks, lockups.

Dividers, room frames, corner marks, eyebrow lockups, counter badges, arrows, the lit full stop and the visitor’s-book rule, in SVG and PNG, two inks. 05_GRAPHIC_ASSETS/Decorative_Elements.

Two inks. --dark (Gallery White / Lamp Gold) for black; --light (Soot / Old Gold / Gold Ink) for Plaster. Never swap them.
The lit stop. The gold point sized to the Fraunces 300 full stop (r ≈ 5.6 % of the font size). The last word may be gold or the stop may be the point — never both.
Live-text templates. The room frames and the wall label keep live <text>; replace the placeholder rect with the project image and edit the three lines. Everything else is outlined.
