Signage specification

Signs, materials and mounting.

This sheet accompanies the artwork in 10_MARKETING_COLLATERAL/Signage. Every sign uses the wordmark or the symbol from 02_LOGO_SYSTEM, Gallery Black backgrounds, Gallery White letters and one gold point. Scaled artwork is marked in the file name (1:10 = 10 % of real size).

Rule: 25 mm cap height per 3 m of viewing distance (≈ 8.5 mm per metre); never below 10 mm on any sign. Cap height is the height of a capital letter, not the point size.

Clear space and minimum sizes

  • Clear space x = ½ the cap height of the wordmark in use, on all sides; nothing enters it.
  • Minimum widths: wordmark 28 mm; horizontal-descriptor lockup 50 mm; vertical stack 22 mm; symbol 6 mm.
  • Arrow icons: monoline, 64/500 stroke, round caps; the gold point at the tail. Arrow box 48 mm on a 300 × 100 plate.

Mounting

  • Plates: four Ø12 mm black stand-offs, 10 mm projection, 12 mm from each corner; or VHB tape on glass and laminate.
  • Door plates: centre line 1500 mm above finished floor, 50 mm from the latch-side architrave.
  • Wayfinding: centre line 1600 mm, at decision points, perpendicular to the route; room numbers beside the door, latch side.
  • Totem: free-standing, weighted base; directory centred 1200–1500 mm.
  • Backdrop: wordmark centre 1700 mm above floor, visible behind standing guests.

Colour and finish

  • Black: Gallery Black 100 K (rich black for solids). White: Gallery White.
  • Gold: one point per sign. Never tints of gold, never gradients, never glow effects in print.
  • Finish matt throughout; no gloss on black panels (fingerprints, reflections).
  • Proof every gold on the chosen substrate before the run.
