03 · Hierarchy and proportions

Seventy, twenty-five, five.

How much of each colour a composition carries, and the seven rules from Colour_Reference.md that keep the system to one light source.

≈ 70 % the roomOne field colour carries the composition: Gallery Black on screen, Plaster on paper. Photography counts as dark when veiled.
≈ 25 % the surfaces and textCards, hairlines, secondary and tertiary text in the neutral steps of the same theme.
≈ 5 % the point of lightGold, once: a dot, an eyebrow, a lit word, a button. Never a fill, never a tint.

01One light source. One gold accent per composition (Lamp Gold on dark, Old Gold on light). Never Lamp Gold and Ochre together.
02Dark is the gallery, cream is the label. Screens, social, video and covers are dark-first. Documents, print and reading surfaces are Plaster / Paper.
03Gold is never body text on dark below 16 px, and on light only as Gold Ink (#8A6124).
04Black is Gallery Black (#000000) on screen and 100 K — or rich black C60 M40 Y40 K100 for large solids — in print.
05Functional colours are for states only (success, warning, error, info); they never decorate.
06No tints of gold. Use opacity on Gallery White (10 %, 6 %) for hairlines on dark and on Soot (10 %, 5.5 %) on light.
07Metallic print. Old Gold may be a metallic spot (nearest metallic Pantone 871 C or 872 C — verify) or foil; on screen the foil gradient is for mockups only.

06 · Gradients

Light that falls off.

Five gradients, all defined in colour-variables.css. None of them is ever applied to type; the foil is a mockup device only.

--sz-gradient-lamp

The glow behind a lit object on black. Hero backgrounds, cover pages, video.

radial-gradient(60% 60% at 50% 40%, rgba(233,178,90,.55) 0%, rgba(233,178,90,.12) 28%, rgba(0,0,0,0) 70%)

--sz-gradient-dusk

Dark section backgrounds, slide backgrounds.

linear-gradient(180deg, #000000 0%, #1C1C1E 100%)

--sz-gradient-plaster

Light document backgrounds, print covers.

linear-gradient(180deg, #FBF8F2 0%, #ECE7DC 100%)

--sz-gradient-foil

Gold-foil simulation for mockups and premium covers only. Never for text under 24 px.

linear-gradient(135deg, #B8863B 0%, #F4C97A 50%, #B8863B 100%)

--sz-gradient-veil

Over photography to carry captions and wall labels.

linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0) 0%, rgba(0,0,0,.72) 100%)

09 · Accessibility and misuse

Readable in every room.

What the palette guarantees for readers, and six things that break it.

Text
  • Primary text pairs are AAA: Gallery White on Gallery Black (19.29:1), Soot on Plaster (15.6:1), Soot on Paper (16.7:1).
  • Secondary text is AAA on dark (Silver 8.16:1) and AA on light (Umber 5.55:1).
  • Tertiary text — Mist on dark, 5.35:1 — is AA; keep it at 12 px or larger and never for running text.
  • Gold Ink on Plaster (4.85:1) is the only gold text on light; Lamp Gold on black (10.98:1) is AAA from 16 px.
Interface and print
  • Hairlines and the focus ring are not the only cue: focus also changes the border colour; states carry a word, not only a colour.
  • Functional colours meet 4.5:1 as text in their own theme (Moss Deep, Ochre Deep, Brick, Slate Deep on Plaster; Moss, Ochre, Signal Red, Slate Blue on black) except Moss Deep as text on Plaster (5.38:1) and Moss on black (5.05:1), both AA.
  • Gallery White on Moss is AA-large only: headlines ≥ 24 px or UI graphics.
  • In print, 100 K text on natural white matches the on-screen pairs; gold foil is never used for words.
