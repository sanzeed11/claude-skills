Motion principles

Light, not bounce. Four curves, four durations.

ease — light
cubic-bezier(.22, .8, .2, 1)
Every entrance and every travel: fast out, long settle. 75 % of the way in the first quarter of the time; the rest is the settle.
ease-in — exits
cubic-bezier(.6, 0, .9, .4)
Only for leaving: a lower third sinking, letters fading out. Things that leave gather speed; they never slam.
ease-io — between two rests
cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .2, 1)
A wipe that must reach the far edge, a glow that pulses once: the move starts from rest and arrives at rest.
ease-loop — breathing
cubic-bezier(.45, 0, .55, 1)
Symmetrical, for seamless loops only: the symbol's point breathing, the loader at rest. Frame last equals frame first.
