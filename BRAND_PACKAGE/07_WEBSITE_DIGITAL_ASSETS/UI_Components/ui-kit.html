Case study · Museums & Experience Centres

Prime Minister Museum

New Delhi

TypeMuseums & Experience Centres
LocationNew Delhi
RoleConcept & creative direction

The brief

One museum for every Prime Minister of India since 1947, in New Delhi — a building that has to hold schoolchildren and heads of state on the same afternoon.

And give each leader a room without turning the whole into a corridor of portraits.

The idea

A corridor you walk in order.

The spine of the museum is a single long passage under a tricolour canopy. Visitors meet one Prime Minister at a time, in sequence, so the building itself tells the story of the office before any gallery does. Portraits face you at eye level; the leaders’ own words sit beside them on the walls.

The walk

Off the spine, each gallery changes its mood to its subject: wood and light for one era, screens and sound for another. Guides stand at the thresholds; planting softens the floor so the passage reads as a walk rather than a hall.

Visitors walk the whole line of leaders without a map, and come out knowing the order by heart.

— What visitors do

Tabs

A hairline rail; the selected tab underlines in the foreground colour with the point at its end. Arrow keys move between tabs.

Case-study parts (live)
A night show for Ayodhya that tells the Ram Katha without a stage or actors — the audience is seated in front of a great hall, and the story has to come out of the architecture itself.
The building is the screen. Every dome, arch and column of the hall was mapped and painted with light, so the story seems to rise from the stone.
Projection, sound and timing were composed as one score: the beats of the narration cue the light, and the architecture’s own rhythm (arch after arch) sets the pace of the scenes.
Visitors sit in the dark and watch a building they know by day become the story by night.

Motion in the UI

Motion is light moving through a room: quick to leave, long to settle. One easing for almost everything, four durations, and the dot that pulses when something is noticed. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Tokens

--easecubic-bezier(.22,.8,.2,1)“Light” — fast out, long settle. Hover, reveal, menus, modals, theme change.
--ease-incubic-bezier(.6,0,.9,.4)Exits, dismissals.
--ease-iocubic-bezier(.65,0,.2,1)Loops: the beam loader, skeleton shimmer, image zoom.
