Radius and line

The room is a rounded rectangle: 28 for cards and frames, 20 for small cards and tiles, 12 for inputs and menus, pill for buttons and tags. Hairlines are white at 10 % on dark, Soot at 10 % on light (6 % / 5.5 % for the quieter line).
