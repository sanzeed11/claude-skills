01 · The three families

Serif speaks, sans works.

Three open-licence families carry the whole identity. Fraunces, at a single light weight, is the voice; Inter Tight structures and labels; Inter is read and tapped. Nothing else is ever added.

Aa

Fraunces

Display and editorial serif. Headlines, statements, stats, pull quotes, long editorial reading. Always 300 Light; italic for quotes.

VARIABLE · opsz 9–144 · wght 100–900 · SOFT · WONK
UNDERCASE TYPE · SIL OFL 1.1
Aa

Inter Tight

Headings, labels, eyebrows, counters and tables. 600 for H3/H4, eyebrows and labels; 500 for data with tabular numerals.

VARIABLE · wght 100–900 · roman + italic
RASMUS ANDERSSON · SIL OFL 1.1
Aa

Inter

Everything that is read: body, lead, captions, wall labels, forms, buttons and navigation. 400 for text, 500 for UI, 600 for emphasis.

Rulesfrom typography-tokens.json
  • Fraunces is always weight 300 (Light); use opsz 144 above 48 px, 72 for 24–48 px, 36 below.
  • Headlines are sentence case and end with a full stop. The last word may be set in gold (.lit) or Mist (.dim) — never both in one composition.
  • Never letter-space Fraunces; never set it below 16 px / 9 pt.
  • Italic is reserved for pull quotes and the personal line.

Case study · Museums & Experience Centres

Prime Minister Museum.

One museum for every Prime Minister of India since 1947, in New Delhi — a building that has to hold schoolchildren and heads of state on the same afternoon.

The idea

A corridor you walk in order. The spine of the museum is a single long passage under a tricolour canopy. Visitors meet one Prime Minister at a time, in sequence, so the building itself tells the story of the office before any gallery does.

What visitors do

Visitors walk the whole line of leaders without a map, and come out knowing the order by heart.

Case study · Projection Mapping & Shows · Ayodhya

The brief

A night show for Ayodhya that tells the Ram Katha without a stage or actors — the audience is seated in front of a great hall, and the story has to come out of the architecture itself.

The idea

Every dome, arch and column of the hall was mapped and painted with light, so the story seems to rise from the stone. Scenes move from the central shrine outwards along the galleries; the gilded ceiling becomes sky, then forest, then fire.

Visitors sit in the dark and watch a building they know by day become the story by night.

What visitors do · Ram Katha Show

The walk

Projection, sound and timing were composed as one score: the beats of the narration cue the light, and the architecture's own rhythm (arch after arch) sets the pace of the scenes.

12 · Rules

Eight rules, kept.

Taken verbatim from typography-tokens.json. They are the whole typographic law of the identity; everything else is layout.

01Headlines are sentence case and end with a full stop.
02The last word of a headline may be set in Lamp Gold (.lit) or Mist (.dim) — never both in one composition.
03Fraunces is always weight 300 (Light); use opsz 144 above 48 px, 72 for 24–48 px, 36 below.
04Inter Tight is for headings/labels (600) and data (500); Inter for everything read.
05Never use Inter Tight below 11 px or Fraunces below 16 px.
06Tabular numerals (tnum) in every table, invoice, quotation and stat.
07Letter-spaced caps only for eyebrows and counters; never letter-space Fraunces.
08Paragraph spacing = 1 line; no indents. Measure 60–72 characters.

What is included
  • Font_Files/<Family>/*.ttf — the original variable TrueType fonts exactly as distributed by Google Fonts. Install these on desktop for InDesign, Illustrator, Word, PowerPoint, Keynote and Figma.
  • Font_Files/<Family>/*-variable.woff2 — web subsets (Latin, Latin Extended-A, general punctuation, ₹, arrows) produced with fontTools for the website design system. Variation axes are preserved.
  • Font_Files/<Family>/OFL.txt — the licence that must travel with the files.
Office documents

Word and PowerPoint templates in this package reference Fraunces, Inter Tight and Inter. Install the TTFs on every machine that edits them; if the fonts are missing, the documents fall back to Georgia and Arial (acceptable for internal drafts, not for client-facing output).

Obligations under the OFL
  1. Keep OFL.txt with the font files when you redistribute them (for example inside a website build or a template pack).
  2. Do not sell the fonts by themselves.
  3. If you modify a font and redistribute it, do not use the Reserved Font Names ("Fraunces", "Inter") for the modified version. The woff2 subsets here are not renamed because subsetting for web embedding is standard practice and they are distributed only as part of this website; if in doubt, serve the original TTFs or Google Fonts' hosted CSS.
