Case study · Museums & Experience Centres

Prime Minister Museum.

One museum for every Prime Minister of India since 1947, in New Delhi — a building that has to hold schoolchildren and heads of state on the same afternoon.

The idea

A corridor you walk in order. The spine of the museum is a single long passage under a tricolour canopy. Visitors meet one Prime Minister at a time, in sequence, so the building itself tells the story of the office before any gallery does.

What visitors do

Visitors walk the whole line of leaders without a map, and come out knowing the order by heart.

Serif speaks. Fraunces carries statements: titles, headlines, stats, quotes, long editorial reading. One serif voice per page.
Sans works. Inter Tight labels and structures; Inter is read and tapped. Neither is ever used for a headline that Fraunces could carry.
Never pair with a third face. No script, no slab, no condensed. The hand-lettered signature is a mark, not a typeface.
