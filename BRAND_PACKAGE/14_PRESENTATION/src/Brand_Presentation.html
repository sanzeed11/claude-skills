03
Three routes considered.
Developed to the same depth before choosing. Route A was chosen.
Three routes, one decision.
Each developed to the same depth before choosing. Route A evolves what already existed; the other two would have replaced it.
Route A
Chosen
The Point of Light.
Evolve and construct what exists: black and cream with the gold accent, the wordmark redrawn as a true geometric construction, the S-and-dot promoted to a brand mark, a graphic language built from the dot, the beam and the room; Fraunces, Inter Tight and Inter kept.
For
Preserves the equity the client invested in · the idea is already in the copy · premium, institutional · survives print, signage and motion.
Against
Less of a "big reveal" · dark-first systems need careful light-mode and print rules — solved in the colour system.
Route B
Not chosen
The Plan.
Architectural drawing language: monochrome ink on paper, blueprint grids, floor-plan diagrams as graphic devices, a signal-red accent, a grotesque typeface.
For
Distinctive in the museum-design category · highly systematic.
Against
Reads as an architecture practice rather than a storyteller · discards the gold "light" idea and the existing wordmark · cold where the brand is warm.
Route C
Not chosen
Spectacle.
Projection-mapping energy: saturated light gradients, motion-blur typography, full-bleed show photography, heavy display type.
For
Dramatic, social-media friendly · communicates the show work instantly.
Against
Contradicts "calm leadership" · dates quickly · weak on paper and in institutional proposals · makes national-museum work look like event marketing.

The idea.

The point, the beam, the room.
Three things, drawn from the wordmark itself. Every asset in the package is made of them and nothing else.
001 The point
A gold dot, r = 0.7 × the stroke of its context. It sits in the A and the D of the wordmark, ends the signature, closes headlines ("lit.") and becomes the symbol. On dark it may glow.
002 The beam
The 1 px hairline the point travels along: the route through a museum, the timeline of a career, the rule in a document, the wipe in motion.
003 The room
A rounded rectangle, radius 28 (20 small), hairline border, Charcoal on dark / Paper on light. Cards, frames, image plates and these slides.

Twelve things never to do.
001 Incorrect usage 02_LOGO_SYSTEM/Guidelines/logo-incorrect-usage.svg
002 Minimum sizes screen and print
003 Placement top-left or centred; documents at 20 mm
Never stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, bevels or glows to the letters (the dots may glow in motion only), recolour the dots a non-gold colour, change the letter spacing, set the name in a typeface instead of the wordmark, pair it with the old raster signature, place the descriptor in another font or case, or crop the S.

Backgrounds: flat brand colours, or photography darkened with the veil so the logo sits on ≥ 80 % black. Never on busy imagery, never on gold (except the black logo on the gold tile), never on gradients other than dusk and lamp.

Fraunces 300
Vision you can walk into.
Light weight only, optical size 144 above 48 px, 72 for 24–48, 36 below; italic for quotes. Never letter-spaced, never below 16 px / 9 pt. Headlines in sentence case ending with a full stop; the last word may be gold.

Inter Tight 600
Immersive Storytelling & Narrative Architecture
Headings that work rather than speak. Eyebrows in caps at +0.12 em in gold; counters 001; Medium 500 with tabular numerals for tables and figures.
Body, captions, UI

Inter 400
I lead the whole room — story, space, graphics, media and the team that builds it.
